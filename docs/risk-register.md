> **REVIEW (owner): review cadence.** Next-review dates below assume a quarterly cadence (next 2026-11-26), except where a deadline forces something sooner. Confirm or set your own.

## How this register works

Risks are seeded from two sources and keep their original identifiers so they can be traced back:

- **`R-C*` / `R-H*` / `R-M*` / `R-L*`** — findings from the security and GDPR audit of 2026-08-12 (`docs/security-audit-2026-08-12.md`), which carries the file-and-line evidence for each.
- **`R-A*`** — items from the ISO 27001-style posture assessment of 2026-08-26, recorded in the merged findings ledger of `docs/plans/2026-08-26-security-hardening-and-platform-ops-plan.md`.
- **`R-N*`** — issues surfaced during remediation work that were never given an audit severity.

**Severity convention.** The severity column reproduces the severity assigned by the source assessment. Nothing here has been re-scored — where the source did not assign a severity, the column says so rather than inventing one.

**Treatment vocabulary.** `Fixed` — the risk is eliminated and verified. `Mitigating` — work is under way or a compensating control is in place, but the risk is not closed. `Accepted` — a decision has been taken to live with the risk. `Open` — acknowledged, no treatment started.

**Acceptance rule.** A risk may be accepted, but never silently: an acceptance must appear in this register and be signed off by the owner. Acceptances marked *"recorded 2026-08-26 — owner sign-off pending"* have been documented but not yet signed.
